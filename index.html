OUR STORY

Born from a
Passion for
the World

Founded in 2012 by Chef Aryan Mehta after 20 years cooking across Europe, Asia and the Americas, Saffron & Silk was born from a singular obsession: to bring the world's most beautiful flavours under one roof without compromise.

Every cuisine is cooked by specialists trained in their native country. Our Italian chef hails from Bologna. Our Japanese chef spent 8 years in Tokyo under a Michelin-starred master. Every plate tells a story of origin, craft and love.
